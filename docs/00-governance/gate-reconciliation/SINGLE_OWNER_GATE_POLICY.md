Earlier WP-000-03 classed specialist, privacy and QA reviewers as mandatory *human G-00 signoffs*. For a one-person design team, propose allowing the owner to accept an **architecture-only freeze** while keeping external expert requirements at service/pilot release. This **material change** requires explicit owner adoption and documented risk treatment. It must not automatically mark earlier hard blockers closed or permit operational use.
