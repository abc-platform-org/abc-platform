## Non-delegable approval rule

An ML model, a developer, an application owner, or a synthetic test result **cannot sign off clinical/cosmetic safety**. The qualified human reviewer must have verified qualifications appropriate for the jurisdiction, explicitly approved scope of practice, evidence of service-specific competency, a recorded conflict-of-interest review, and authority from the salon's governance owner. The reviewer must inspect the actual service and materials, not merely the abstract AI plan.

No caller-supplied `approved=true`, role string, fake token, test fixture or model inference is an approval. `ENG14ProfessionalGate.assess()` must continue returning `BLOCK` until independently verified identity, consent and policy signatures are transactionally bound to the exact session + service + selected-look version + evidence + SOP version.

**Important:** risk tiers above are *engineering proposals*, not approved clinical standards or medical instructions. Reviewers may reclassify up, never silently down. Additional jurisdiction-specific rules and age/guardian consent must be evaluated.

## Approval receipt contract (for future secure implementation)

Each approval must be generated by a verified external professional/governance issuer and include:

- `approval_id` (UUID), `issuer`, `reviewer_subject`, `verified_qualification_ref`, `jurisdiction`, `role`
- `tenant_id`, `branch_id`, `session_id`, `client_consent_evidence_id`, `service_id`
- `selected_look_version`, `evidence_snapshot_digest`, `plan_version`, `sop_id`, `sop_version`
- `decision` restricted to `APPROVED | CONDITIONALLY_APPROVED | REJECTED`; conditions and stop criteria as typed records
- `iat`, `exp`, `jti`, issuer key ID and verifiable signature; revocation status and audit timestamp

The approval expires immediately on evidence or look-version change, SOP revocation, change to professional authorization, consent revocation, branch or tenant change, or stop-criterion activation. Conditional approval does **not** authorize missing required evidence or an unapproved technique.

## Hard implementation boundaries

1. Server-side identity + revocation and qualification registries; never a request-owned reviewer ID.
2. Consent is purpose-scoped and time-valid; no photo analysis, storage, handoff or processing can proceed on a guessed or generic consent.
3. ENG-14 must independently evaluate every state transition in the same database transaction as the state update; a front-end green status is not authoritative.
4. Source lineage, confidence/unknown state and professional findings must be preserved without AI-generated substitutions.
5. ENG-06 Technical Roadmap remains **draft, not a client-ready treatment instruction** until the selected-look, material/safety evidence and qualified human signoff are all verified.
6. Implement independently testable `DENY` results for stale approval, mismatched service/tenant/branch, wrong SOP, reused jti, revoked consent, expired qualification, forged key and changed selected-look version.
