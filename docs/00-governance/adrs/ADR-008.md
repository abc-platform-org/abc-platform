# ADR-008 — SOP grounded technical roadmap and specialist signoff

**Status:** `PROPOSED_FOR_REVIEW` | **Date:** 2026-10-28 | **Decision maker:** to assign at Gate-01

## Context

LLM-generated procedure without validated evidence can be wrong/dangerous.

## Proposed decision

Conditional procedure graph bound to approved SOP and exact look; human validation recorded.

## Consequences and tradeoffs

Requires vetted SOPs and expert review per service.

## Alternatives still to assess

Consider minimal platform alternatives and vendor capability through a POC; no implementation is claimed by this decision document. No single provider is mandatory without benchmark evidence.

## Affected contracts

`AIS-023,AIS-024`; update relevant schemas, engine tests, migration map and risk register before marking this ADR accepted.

## Verification and exit

Demonstrate contract test(s), integration flow and negative/failure cases appropriate to this decision. Capture code SHA, test execution result, human reviewer and linked issue/PR; status remains PROPOSED until review.

## WP-000-02 — Technical planning safety contract (2026-10-29)
**Status remains `PROPOSED_FOR_REVIEW`.** Plan generation occurs **after** exact `LookVersion` selection and service feasibility review. Engine 15 drafts a conditional, SOP-linked procedure graph; Engine 16 alone records verified specialist validation. Plan inputs: source-proven evidence, `sop_version`, current state, verified professional findings, materials/availability, safety gates. Output: objectives, prerequisites, conditional stages, controls, stop conditions, aftercare, unknowns and draft status.
**Prohibited:** invented formula, mixture, processing time or dosage; turning preview QA PASS into executable authorization; commercial override of safety. Missing SOP/history or contradictory evidence => advisory-only / AWAITING_SPECIALIST_INPUT / STOP. **Version binding:** Look, SOP or relevant evidence change invalidates dependent approval. **Alternatives rejected:** freeform LLM instructions, automatic execution approval. **Tests:** TEC-01..05, negative chemical/red-team cases, specialist signature/authorization. **Approval:** qualified specialist/SOP and safety reviewers, not inferred from Product signoff.
