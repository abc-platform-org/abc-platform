# ABC Master Product Charter — Proposed Rebaseline

Document: `ABC-CHARTER-001` | status: `PROPOSED` | date: 2026-10-28

## Mission

Create a specialist-operated salon platform enabling an in-person client and professional to understand beauty goals, ask adaptive service-aware questions, inspect observations and references, produce grounded style options, visualize accurately bounded changes on the **client's own image**, compare and iterate, select a versioned look, conduct feasibility checks, build a protocol-grounded technical execution roadmap, obtain genuine specialist validation, and hand off relevant results to real salon operations.

## Product domains

1. **Core intelligence:** adaptive consultation, evidence, beauty analysis, ontology, recommendation, look specification.
2. **AI Studio:** controlled transformation, try-on, comparison, inspiration, image quality, segmentation, compositing, QA, versions, history, shared-screen interaction.
3. **Professional execution:** feasibility, safety, SOPs, conditional steps, materials, quality checkpoints, sign-off, handoff.
4. **Salon Business OS:** home, global search, calendar, clients, team, services, sales/POS, inventory, marketing, analytics, automations, reviews, notifications, branches, settings, Ask ABC.

## Non-negotiable principles

- An attractive AI image is **not** evidence that a physical result is feasible.
- Choice by a client is **not** specialist approval; AI may not self-approve.
- Recommendations must honor explicit exclusions and safety / feasibility constraints ahead of promotions.
- Unknown evidence stays unknown. No fabricated booking, prices, stock, history, procedure parameters, or service availability.
- Visual edits affect only explicitly authorized regions; approved unrelated state is preserved; QA failures remain failures.
- Any execution-relevant chemical formula, timing or material instruction requires validated product/SOP and professional review.
- Shared display is not a public interface to management/private data.
- Each engine is independently specified, testable, versioned, observable, and failure-aware; not merely an instruction paragraph.
- Source history is preserved; legacy GPTs-only amendments are historical evidence, not the target runtime.
- No claim of implementation, pass-rate, compliance, or live financial metrics without verifiable evidence.

## Explicit non-goals

No autonomous execution of beauty procedures; no medical diagnosis; no guaranteed visual/physical outcomes; no customer home self-service or automatic remote preview delivery; no unreviewed financial transactions; no assumption that every advanced service is released in MVP.

## Decision and release policy

Feature existence is not equal to capability maturity. Lifecycle: `SPECIFIED → CONTRACTED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → PROFESSIONALLY_REVIEWED (where relevant) → RELEASED`. Changing a configuration flag does not bypass these states. All thresholds are `TBD_AFTER_EMPIRICAL_CALIBRATION` until approved.
